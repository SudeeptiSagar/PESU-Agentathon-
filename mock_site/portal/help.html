Disability Pension Portal (mock)

Who can apply

Any resident with a certified disability of 40 percent or more and an annual family income below Rs 3,00,000.

Filling the form

  • The form has two tabs: Personal Details and Bank Details. Fill both.
  • Mobile number must be 10 digits. PIN code must be 6 digits. Aadhaar must be 12 digits.
  • IFSC is 11 characters: 4 letters, a zero, then 6 letters or digits (for example SBIN0001234).
  • Tick the verification box before you submit.

After you submit

You will see an acknowledgment number. Keep it to track your application.
