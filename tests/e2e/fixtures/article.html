Kalpana Chawla

Kalpana Chawla (17 March 1962 - 1 February 2003) was an Indian-born American astronaut and aerospace engineer.

Early life

Kalpana Chawla was born in Karnal, Haryana, India. She was the first woman of Indian origin to go to space.

Career

She flew on Space Shuttle Columbia in 1997 and again in 2003.
